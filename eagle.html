FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I have to leave WordPress?

No. You can start with Eagle connected to your existing WordPress site and switch to your new site later, or switch straight away. It's the same plan either way.

What can Eagle change on my WordPress site?

Pages, posts and FAQs; your opening hours or phone number on every page at once; photos and their descriptions; menus; and SEO titles and descriptions. Every change can be undone through WordPress's own revision history. Eagle never touches your theme or plugin code, and anything involving your shop or prices always needs your approval.

Why move off WordPress at all?

A modern static site is faster, has far fewer plugins and updates to break, and is easier for Eagle and AI tools to read. Most small business sites don't need anything WordPress-specific.

Who owns the hosting and the website?

You do. Your developer puts the site on hosting in your name, your domain stays registered to you, and the code lives in your own GitHub account. If you cancel Eagle, the site keeps running.

Who puts the site live?

Your developer. Eagle sends them everything they need - the code, a launch guide and the redirect list - in one email you forward. Eagle doesn't do hosting or setup, so technical questions go to someone who knows your business.

Why WhatsApp?

Because you already use it all day. Eagle sends you what needs doing with simple buttons, and you can change your site just by texting. The dashboard is there when you want the full picture.

I already have a developer. Do I still need Eagle?

Eagle does the day-to-day work - small edits, review replies, content, posts - so your developer can focus on the jobs that need them. Invite them as a guest and they can put the site live, see the technical details and propose changes.

What if I change developer?

Remove the old developer from your dashboard in one click and invite the new one. They get a handover pack with everything they need. Your site, code, history and Eagle stay exactly as they are.

What if I want something bigger, like online booking?

Eagle handles content and everyday changes. If you ask for new functionality like booking or a shop, Eagle tells you it's outside the plan and writes it up as a clear job for your developer.

Will Eagle help me get more reviews?

Yes. Eagle drafts review requests with your Google and Trustpilot links and gives you a QR code for in-person asks. It won't offer rewards for reviews or hide unhappy customers - that breaks Google's and Trustpilot's rules and could get your listing penalised.

How do I handle new enquiries?

Every enquiry from your website, Google, WhatsApp and email shows up in one list with three buttons: call back, send reply or done. Eagle drafts the reply for you and reminds you if anyone is still waiting at the end of the day.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Eagle is built for busy owners, not techies: one screen, big buttons and plain English. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can run Eagle.

Can anything go live without me?

No. Every change, page, reply and post waits for your approval, whether you approve it in the dashboard or on WhatsApp.

How is Eagle different from Wren?

Wren is WingLift's AI visibility audit: it tells you what AI tools say about your business. Eagle includes Wren's findings and goes further - it gives you a finished site and does the work to fix what Wren finds, with your approval.

Do you give me an "AI visibility score"?

No. Scores hide what matters. Eagle shows you exactly what each AI tool says, what's wrong, which competitors appear instead of you, and what to do about it.
