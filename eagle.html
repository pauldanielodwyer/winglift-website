FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I have to leave WordPress?

No. You can start with Eagle connected to your existing WordPress site and move to your own fast site later, or move straight away. It's the same plan either way.

Why move off WordPress at all?

A modern static site on Cloudflare is faster, has far fewer plugins and updates to break, and is easier for Eagle and AI tools to read. Most small business sites don't need anything WordPress-specific.

Who owns the hosting and the website?

You do. The site is published to a Cloudflare account in your name, your domain stays registered to you, and you can download the code any time. If you cancel Eagle, the site keeps running.

I already have a developer. Do I still need Eagle?

Eagle does the day-to-day work - small edits, review replies, content, posts - so your developer can focus on the jobs that need them. Invite them as a guest and they can run the migration, see the technical details and propose changes.

Can anything go live without me?

No. Every change, page, reply and post waits for your approval, whether you approve it in the dashboard or on WhatsApp.

How is Eagle different from Wren?

Wren is WingLift's AI visibility audit: it tells you what AI tools say about your business. Eagle includes Wren's findings and goes further - it gives you a finished site and does the work to fix what Wren finds, with your approval.

Do you give me an "AI visibility score"?

No. Scores hide what matters. Eagle shows you exactly what each AI tool says, what's wrong, which competitors appear instead of you, and what to do about it.
