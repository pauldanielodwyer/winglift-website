How it works

01

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Will you rebuild or host my website?

No. Eagle is software only. Your website stays where it is, with the hosting and developer you already have. We never ask for your code.

Does my site need to be on WordPress?

No. On WordPress, Eagle can publish approved edits for you through the WordPress REST API. On any other platform, Eagle gives you or your developer the exact copy-paste change instead.

Can anything go live without me?

No. Every website edit, FAQ, social post and review reply waits in your approval queue until you approve it, in the dashboard or on WhatsApp.

What does my developer do?

The code work, as they do today. Eagle gives them a prioritised list of technical fixes from the AI audit and lets them propose changes for your approval. They're invited as a guest and never see billing.

How is Eagle different from the Wren audit?

Wren tells you what AI says about your business. Eagle uses those findings and helps you act on them: it drafts the fixes, manages your FAQs, reviews and social posts, and publishes what you approve.

Do you give me an “AI visibility score”?

No. Scores hide the detail. Eagle shows you exactly what each AI says, what it gets wrong, and who it recommends instead.
